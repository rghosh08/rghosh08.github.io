Professional Summary

Technical leader with 12 years of expertise in reinforcement learning, optimal control, Large Language Models (LLM), AI safety, and retrieval augmented generation (RAG). Demonstrated success in architecting scalable, impactful AI-driven applications and leading multidisciplinary teams.

He joined Nutanix in 2021 as a Staff Scientist to establish and lead advanced AI research, including Reinforcement Learning, across the product portfolio, bringing startup experience rooted in his PhD work. He played a foundational role in Nutanix’s early generative AI efforts, architecting SupportGPT and defining its core RAG design and evaluation framework at a time when open-source LLMs were still immature, ultimately driving the system to production-grade quality. Rajat has also provided deep technical leadership in AI safety, alignment, and reasoning-model fine-tuning, influencing the NAI roadmap and AI-efficiency initiatives. Beyond product innovation, he is a trusted technical leader across the company and with customers—leading the evaluation and adoption of Cursor as Nutanix’s coding assistant, serving as the SME for legal reviews of AI tools and LLMs, supporting the conversion of a key healthcare customer to NAI 1.0, and partnering with Corporate Development on AI technical due diligence—while building a strong AI research team and contributing to industry thought leadership through peer-reviewed publications, invited conference panels, internal talks, and Nutanix AI/ML blogs.
