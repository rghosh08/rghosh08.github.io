Professional Summary

I specialize in converting high-ambiguity, frontier AI problems into trusted, scalable platforms used across large engineering organizations. These systems—including internal coding agents, support agents, and post-training pipelines—are adopted by hundreds of engineers and SREs across multiple teams and geographies, with usage, accuracy, and cost metrics tracked in production. My scope has consistently extended beyond model quality to include evaluation rigor, safety and alignment, legal readiness, infrastructure efficiency, and operational scalability. These efforts established reusable platforms and standards rather than one-off solutions, enabling multiple product teams to build on shared foundations. I operate with high leverage through people. I have built and led multi-disciplinary teams across research, ML engineering, and infrastructure, while mentoring interns and senior ICs who converted to high-impact hires or moved on to top AI labs. I established execution rhythms—clear OKRs, weekly stakeholder reviews, EA/GA rollout discipline, and metric-driven iteration—that allowed teams to scale impact while maintaining quality and trust. Cross-functional leadership is a core part of my role. I regularly partner with Product, Legal, Security, Infra, and executive leadership to unblock adoption of AI systems in enterprise settings. I have led legal fast-track reviews for AI tools, enterprise risk assessments, and procurement decisions, and I am frequently trusted to represent technical positions in VP-level and exec-level forums. Beyond internal impact, I maintain external technical credibility. I have published and presented work at leading ML venues (NeurIPS, ICLR, AAAI workshops), hold multiple patents in LLM alignment, code intelligence, and benchmarking, and actively contribute to open-source and industry initiatives such as MLCommons and PyTorch. This external engagement informs my internal technical judgment and helps align platform decisions with broader industry trajectories.
